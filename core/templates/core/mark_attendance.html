How this page works

The attendance register records which learners came to each programme session. It follows the same layout as the Inkululeko attendance spreadsheet: one row per learner, each month split into weeks, and a box for every school day from Monday to Friday.

Reading the register

  • Each row is one learner. The left side shows their learner ID, name, grade and school. These columns stay in place while you scroll sideways through the dates. Only active learner accounts are listed.
  • Each month is split into weeks. Weeks follow the real calendar, so a month can have four or five weeks, and the first or last week of a month may only have a few days. Grey boxes belong to the neighbouring month.
  • The column after Friday shows that week’s percentage for each learner.
  • Today’s column is highlighted. Days that haven’t happened yet are faded and can’t be ticked.
  • Hatched columns are days with no session, such as school holidays and public holidays. Hover over or select the date to see why.
  • Dashed boxes are days before a learner’s start date (see below). They don’t count against the learner, but you can still tick them if the learner attended.

Taking attendance

Tick a learner’s box on a day they were present. Leave it empty if they were absent. There is no separate “absent” mark: once a session day has passed, an empty box counts as absent. Each tick is saved to the database straight away, so everyone using the register sees the same information. The note in the bottom-right corner of the register shows whether your changes have been saved. If a change can’t be saved, for example because the connection dropped, the box goes back to how it was and a message explains what happened.

To mark a whole day at once, select the date at the top of its column and choose Mark all shown as present or Mark all shown as absent. This only changes the learners currently shown, so you can filter to one school or grade first and mark just that group. You can then untick the few learners who were missing.

How the numbers are worked out

  • Session days are weekdays up to and including today that aren’t marked as having no session.
  • A learner’s start date is the earlier of the day their account was created and the first day they were ever ticked as present. Session days before that aren’t counted, so a learner who joins in August isn’t marked absent for February to July. If you enter attendance for earlier days, the start date moves back to the first day you tick.
  • Total days is the number of session days in the chosen year from the learner’s start date to today.
  • Present days is the number of those days the learner was ticked as present.
  • Attendance is present days divided by total days. The same figure appears on dashboards, learner pages, the attendance report and report cards.
  • The weekly percentage is the days present that week divided by the session days that week. A week with a holiday is only measured against the days that actually had a session. A dash means there were no session days for that learner that week.
  • Colours make it easier to spot learners who need support: green is 80% or more, amber is 60% to 79%, and red is below 60%.

The summary line above the register covers the learners currently shown. Average attendance is the combined present days of those learners divided by their combined total days, and Below 60% counts how many of them are under that threshold.

Holidays and closures

Days with no session don’t count towards anyone’s total, so learners aren’t marked down for holidays. The 2026 register starts with the public school holidays from the Department of Basic Education calendar, South African public holidays that fall on a weekday, and January and December closed for the programme. Other years start empty, so add their holidays before taking attendance.

Administrators can change these. Classroom assistants can see the list but not change it.

  • To close a single day, select its date and choose Mark as no session. You can do this in advance for days that haven’t happened yet.
  • To reopen a single day, select its date and choose Hold a session on this day.
  • To manage whole periods, use Holidays and closures above the register. It lists every closure with its dates, lets you remove any of them, and lets you add a new one with a name and a date range.

Closing a day permanently removes any ticks already on it. Reopening a day that has already passed means it counts again, so every learner will show as absent for that day until they are ticked.

Views and filters

  • Month shows one month at a time. Use the arrows to move between months. This view is easiest on a phone.
  • Full year shows all twelve months side by side, like the spreadsheet, with an orange divider between months. It opens at the current month.
  • Year, at the top right, switches to another year’s register.
  • School, grade and name filters narrow the list of learners. You can search by name or by learner ID.
